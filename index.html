Our Story

A club built around the life and love of the game

The Monture Court began with a simple observation: padel was growing faster than the spaces built to hold it. Courts were treated as commodities, not as places people actually wanted to spend their evenings.

We set out to build something closer to a social court than a sports facility. Considered architecture, a kitchen worth staying for, and a calendar that treats clinics, leagues, and long dinners as equally essential parts of membership.
